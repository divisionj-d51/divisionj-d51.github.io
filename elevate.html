Division J ELEVATE · Aug 2026 – Jun 2027

Leadership skills from world-class programs. Free, online, yours.

ELEVATE is Division J's flagship development series: 22 Zoom sessions on career and leadership growth, each built on practices from named corporate programs and integrating AI tools into skills you can use at work the next day.

Register your interest See the session calendar

How ELEVATE works

Three commitments define every session. Format: Intro (5 min) → Core content (35 min) → Activity/practice (15 min) → Q&A (5 min).

Sourcing

Real programs, named openly

Every session is anchored to a recognized corporate development program — from Amazon, Google, McKinsey, Microsoft, GE, Harvard, and others — not generic "best practices".

Technology

AI tools in every session

Hands-on use of tools such as Claude, Perplexity, Notion AI, Gamma, Otter.ai, Canva AI, and Grammarly — applied to the session's skill, not demonstrated in the abstract.

Application

Career-focused, not organization-focused

ELEVATE teaches universally applicable career and leadership skills you can use in any workplace — it is personal development, delivered by Toastmasters volunteers.
